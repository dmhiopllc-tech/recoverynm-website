Recovery NM • Treatment Scholarships

Need Help Paying for Addiction Treatment?

Recovery NM works to reduce financial barriers to substance use treatment for New Mexico residents. If you or someone you care about needs assistance, we're here to help you understand how to ask about available scholarship support.

Ask About Scholarship Assistance
A Path Toward Care

Financial Barriers Shouldn't Close the Door to Recovery.

Recovery NM raises funds to support eligible treatment costs at qualified New Mexico treatment providers. Scholarships depend on available funding, program requirements, and individual circumstances.

For New Mexico Residents

Our scholarship mission focuses on people living in New Mexico who face financial barriers to substance use treatment.

Different Treatment Options

Depending on program requirements, scholarship support may help with outpatient, residential, or other eligible substance use treatment services.

Provider Choice

Recovery NM's mission extends beyond any single treatment center and supports access to qualified providers in New Mexico.

How to Get Started

Three Simple Steps

1

Contact Recovery NM

Send a brief email to let us know you would like information about treatment scholarship assistance. Please don't include medical details.

2

Discuss Next Steps

Recovery NM can explain the current inquiry process and any applicable requirements or available opportunities.

3

Explore Available Support

Any scholarship consideration depends on eligibility, available funding, and the program's requirements. Contacting us does not guarantee an award.

Get in Touch

Ask About Treatment Scholarship Assistance

You can begin by emailing Recovery NM. We'll provide information about the next steps and current scholarship availability.

For your privacy, include only your name, preferred contact information, and a request to discuss scholarship assistance. Please do not send diagnoses, medical records, insurance identifiers, or treatment histories by ordinary email.

This is an initial information request, not a scholarship application or confirmation of funding.
