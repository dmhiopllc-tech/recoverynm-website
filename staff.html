Our Story & Leadership

New Mexicans Helping New Mexicans.

Recovery NM was created around a simple belief: financial barriers should not be the reason someone loses an opportunity to pursue substance use treatment.

Meet Our Leadership Support the Mission
Why Recovery NM Exists

Turning Community Support Into Treatment Opportunity.

Recovery NM was founded to help New Mexico residents who face financial barriers to substance use treatment.

Our mission is focused on raising charitable support and using those resources to help create treatment scholarship opportunities for New Mexicans who might otherwise struggle to access care.

Recovery NM is not built around one treatment center. Our purpose is broader: to support access to treatment and work with the recovery community so that available resources can help New Mexico residents pursue appropriate care.

Recovery is personal. Access to treatment should not depend solely on someone's ability to pay.
