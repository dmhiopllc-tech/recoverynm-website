Our Team

Dedicated to making recovery accessible to all New Mexicans

Meet Our Team

Recovery NM is led by a dedicated team of professionals committed to breaking down financial barriers to substance abuse treatment. Our board and staff work tirelessly to ensure that every New Mexico resident has access to the care they need to achieve lasting recovery.

Leadership Team

👤

Gary Gamboa

President

As a lifelong New Mexican, Gary Gamboa understands the unique challenges facing our communities. As President of Recovery NM, Gary oversees the organization's strategic direction and ensures that our mission of making treatment accessible remains at the forefront of everything we do.

His deep roots in New Mexico and his commitment to serving fellow New Mexicans drive his passion for helping families affected by substance abuse. Gary's leadership ensures that Recovery NM stays focused on what matters most—connecting our neighbors with the life-saving care they need.

👤

Sean Roberts

Vice President

Sean Roberts serves as Vice President of Recovery NM, bringing extensive experience in treatment coordination and community outreach. Sean's passion for helping individuals and families navigate the path to recovery drives his work connecting New Mexico residents with the treatment resources they desperately need.

His hands-on approach and dedication to building partnerships with treatment providers across the state ensure that Recovery NM scholarships reach those who need them most. Sean's compassionate leadership helps transform lives every day.

Board & Staff

👤

Fred Gamboa

Board Member at Large

Fred Gamboa serves as Board Member at Large, bringing valuable perspective and community connections to Recovery NM's mission. His commitment to strengthening New Mexico communities and supporting those in need of recovery services helps guide the organization's growth and impact.

Fred's dedication to transparency, accountability, and effective stewardship ensures that Recovery NM operates with integrity and maximizes the impact of every donation received.

👤

Tatiana Schnierow

Office Manager

Tatiana Schnierow serves as Office Manager, ensuring the smooth day-to-day operations of Recovery NM. Her organizational skills and attention to detail keep the organization running efficiently so that scholarship applications are processed quickly and families get help when they need it most.

Tatiana's warm and compassionate approach makes her often the first point of contact for families seeking assistance. Her dedication to helping others and her commitment to excellent service embody the heart of Recovery NM's mission.
