Community Makes the Mission Possible

Recovery Takes a Community.

Recovery NM is strengthened by organizations, public partners, treatment providers, donors, volunteers, and community members who believe New Mexicans should have a meaningful opportunity to access substance use treatment.

Meet Our Partners Become a Partner
With Gratitude

Thank You for Standing Behind Recovery.

Recovery NM's work is supported by people and organizations willing to invest in treatment access and recovery opportunities for New Mexico residents.

Financial contributions, community collaboration, referrals, outreach, and organizational support all help strengthen our ability to pursue the mission of reducing financial barriers to substance use treatment.

Every partnership represents a shared belief that people seeking recovery deserve an opportunity to get help.
Featured Community Support

Organizations Helping Recovery NM Move the Mission Forward

We are grateful for the support of organizations that have contributed funding, partnership, and community commitment to Recovery NM.

🏦
Major Donor

Nusenda Credit Union

Recovery NM is grateful to Nusenda Credit Union for its generous financial support of our treatment scholarship mission. Its contribution helped turn community investment into direct access to care for New Mexico residents facing financial barriers to treatment.

Verified impact: Nusenda Credit Union's support helped Recovery NM fund treatment for two New Mexico clients.
🏛️
Government Partner

Sandoval County

Recovery NM extends its gratitude to Sandoval County and the Sandoval County Commissioners for supporting recovery resources and community wellness. Public-sector partnership helps strengthen the network of organizations working to improve access to help for New Mexico residents.

We value Sandoval County's commitment to its residents and its support of efforts that expand opportunities for recovery.

🤝
Founding Partner

Desert Mountain Healing IOP

Desert Mountain Healing IOP has supported Recovery NM's mission and its commitment to improving access to substance use treatment. We recognize its role as a founding community partner and its continued commitment to recovery and community wellness.
