Our Community Partners

Thank You for Making Recovery Possible

A Heartfelt Thank You

Recovery NM exists because of the generosity and compassion of our community partners, donors, and supporters. Your contributions directly fund scholarships that give New Mexico residents access to life-changing substance abuse treatment.

Every donation, whether large or small, represents hope, healing, and a second chance at life. Together, we are building a stronger, healthier New Mexico—one person, one family, one community at a time.

Thank you for believing in our mission and for standing with those who need it most.

Special Thanks to Our Major Supporters

🏦 Nusenda Credit Union

A heartfelt thank you to Nusenda Credit Union for their generous donation that has helped countless New Mexico residents on their road to recovery. Your commitment to our community goes beyond financial support—it represents hope, compassion, and a brighter future for those struggling with substance abuse.

Through your partnership, families are being reunited, lives are being rebuilt, and our community is becoming stronger. Thank you for believing in second chances and for investing in the health and wellness of New Mexicans.
