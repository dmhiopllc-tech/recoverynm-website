Our Community Mission

Building Connection, Compassion, and Hope.

Recovery NM is a New Mexico 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to bringing people together through charitable support and community partnerships. We believe a stronger community begins when neighbors listen, learn, and look out for one another.
