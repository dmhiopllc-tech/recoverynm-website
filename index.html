Our Mission

Breaking Down Barriers to Recovery

Recovery NM exists to remove the financial barriers that prevent New Mexico residents from accessing life-saving substance abuse treatment. Every day, individuals and families face the devastating reality that quality treatment is often out of reach due to cost.

We believe that no one should be denied the chance at recovery because of their financial situation. Through our scholarship program, we provide direct financial assistance to New Mexico residents who need help covering the cost of treatment at qualified New Mexico treatment facilities.

New Mexicans Helping New Mexicans

We're proud to connect New Mexico residents with exceptional New Mexico treatment providers—keeping care local, building stronger communities, and ensuring help is close to home. When you choose recovery with Recovery NM, you're not just getting treatment—you're joining a statewide network of compassionate professionals who understand our unique challenges and culture.

Whether you're seeking help for yourself or a loved one, hope is here. Recovery is possible. And we're ready to help you take the first step.

The Financial Barrier

Many New Mexico residents desperately need substance abuse treatment but face an impossible financial barrier. Insurance often doesn't cover the full cost of quality care, and out-of-pocket expenses can reach thousands of dollars—money that most families simply don't have.

This is where Recovery NM steps in. We believe that financial circumstances should never determine whether someone gets the help they need to save their life. Our scholarship program removes this barrier and opens the door to recovery for those who would otherwise go without treatment.

Our Scholarship Program

Who Can Apply?

Our scholarship program is open to New Mexico residents who need financial assistance to access substance abuse treatment. We work with individuals and families from all backgrounds, income levels, and circumstances.

  • Must be a New Mexico resident
  • Seeking treatment at a qualified facility
  • Demonstrable financial need
  • Committed to recovery and personal growth
  • Willing to participate in program requirements

What Does the Scholarship Cover?

Recovery NM scholarships help cover the cost of evidence-based treatment services including:

  • Intensive Outpatient Programs (IOP) – Structured treatment while living at home
  • Residential Treatment – Immersive care in a supportive environment
  • Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) – Evidence-based pharmacological support
  • Individual & Group Therapy – Professional counseling and peer support
  • Aftercare Planning – Ongoing support for sustained recovery

How to Apply

Getting help starts with a simple conversation. Contact us to discuss your situation, and we'll work with you to:

  1. Understand your treatment needs and financial situation
  2. Connect you with qualified treatment providers
  3. Determine scholarship eligibility and funding availability
  4. Complete a simple application process
  5. Get you started on your recovery journey

Don't let cost stand in the way of recovery. Call us at (505) 270-0840 or email us to start the conversation today.
