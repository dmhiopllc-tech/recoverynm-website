Our Mission

When Someone Is Ready for Recovery, Cost Shouldn't Close the Door.

Recovery NM exists to help remove financial barriers that can prevent New Mexico residents from accessing substance use treatment. We raise funds for treatment scholarships so people can pursue care at qualified treatment providers in New Mexico.

No one should be denied the chance at recovery because of their financial situation.
How Recovery NM Helps

From Generosity to Treatment Access

Our purpose is straightforward: turn community support into opportunities for New Mexicans to access treatment.

Your Donation at Work

Your Gift Becomes Access to Treatment.

Donations to Recovery NM support treatment scholarships for New Mexico residents facing financial barriers to care.

  • Intensive outpatient treatment
  • Residential treatment
  • Medication-assisted treatment
  • Individual and group therapy
  • Aftercare and continuing recovery support when included in an approved scholarship
