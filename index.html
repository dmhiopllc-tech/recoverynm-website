Our Mission

Breaking Down Barriers to Recovery

Recovery NM exists to remove the financial barriers that prevent New Mexico residents from accessing life-saving substance abuse treatment. Every day, individuals and families face the devastating reality that quality treatment is often out of reach due to cost.

We believe that no one should be denied the chance at recovery because of their financial situation. Through our scholarship program, we provide direct financial assistance to New Mexico residents who need help covering the cost of treatment at qualified facilities.

Building Connections That Matter

We partner with community organizations, treatment centers, and support groups across New Mexico to ensure everyone has access to the resources they need. Whether you're seeking help for yourself or a loved one, we're here to help you find your path forward.

How Your Donations Make a Difference

Every dollar you donate directly funds treatment scholarships for New Mexico residents struggling with substance abuse.

💰 Scholarship Funding

Your donations fund scholarships that cover treatment costs at qualified New Mexico facilities. We work directly with treatment centers to ensure every dollar goes toward helping someone access care.

🏥 Treatment Access

We help individuals and families afford intensive outpatient programs, residential treatment, medication-assisted treatment, and other evidence-based recovery services.

❤️ Lives Changed

Each scholarship represents a person who gets a second chance at life, a family reunited, and a community strengthened. Your support creates lasting change across New Mexico.

The Recovery NM Difference

Unlike other organizations, 100% of your donation goes directly to funding treatment scholarships. We keep overhead minimal so that every contribution has maximum impact on the lives of those seeking recovery.

Thanks to the generous support of community partners like Nusenda Credit Union and Sandoval County Commissioners, we can ensure your donation reaches those who need it most.
