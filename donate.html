Why Your Donation Matters

Cost Can Become a Barrier at the Exact Moment Someone Is Ready for Help.

Treatment needs differ from person to person, but the financial challenge can affect access at many points in the recovery process.

Assessment & Intake

Entering care often begins with professional assessment and intake. Scholarship assistance can help remove financial barriers to beginning treatment.

Outpatient Treatment

Structured outpatient and intensive outpatient services can provide counseling, education, accountability, and ongoing clinical support while a person remains in the community.

Residential Treatment

Some people may need a higher level of care. Scholarship support may help eligible recipients access approved treatment services based on available funding and program requirements.
