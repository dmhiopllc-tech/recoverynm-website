Make a Donation

Your gift provides hope, healing, and a second chance at life

Why Your Donation Matters

Every donation to Recovery NM directly funds treatment scholarships for New Mexico residents who cannot afford substance abuse treatment. Your generosity removes the financial barriers that keep people from getting the help they desperately need.

Your Donation Goes Directly to Those in Need

Unlike many organizations, we keep administrative costs minimal. Every dollar you donate is maximized to fund life-saving treatment for New Mexico residents. Thanks to the generous support of community partners like Nusenda Credit Union and Sandoval County Commissioners, your contribution has the greatest possible impact on those who need it most.

The Reality of Treatment Costs

Quality substance abuse treatment is life-saving—but it's also expensive. Many New Mexico families face impossible choices when treatment costs reach thousands of dollars per month. Your donation removes these financial barriers and opens the door to recovery.

💰 Assessment & Intake

Professional assessment and intake services are the critical first step—but many families can't afford even this initial cost. Your donation opens the door to hope.

⏱️ Complete IOP Journey

A full 3-month Intensive Outpatient Program represents tens of thousands of dollars—an impossible burden for most families. Your donation provides comprehensive treatment that saves families and rebuilds futures.

🏡 Residential Treatment

30 days of residential treatment in a safe, supportive environment with round-the-clock care and medical supervision is often the most expensive—and most effective—path to recovery. Your donation makes it accessible.

❤️ Every Gift Makes a Difference

Treatment costs shouldn't determine who gets to recover. Whether you give $50 or $5,000, your donation directly helps New Mexico residents access the care they desperately need.

Your Gift Changes Lives

When insurance won't cover treatment and families have exhausted their resources, Recovery NM scholarships become the difference between despair and hope. Your donation is someone's second chance at life.
